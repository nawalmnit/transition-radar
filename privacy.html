Privacy Policy — Transition Radar

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Transition Radar is a skills-only plugin for ChatGPT and Codex. It does not operate its own external user account system, advertising system, analytics database, or independent user-data store.

Information processed

When you use Transition Radar, information you provide in your ChatGPT or Codex interaction may be processed by the host platform to provide the requested experience. The plugin instructions may cause the host to analyze information supplied in the conversation and, where available and appropriate, use current research sources.

External services

The plugin itself does not require you to create an account with a separate Transition Radar service. Any information handled by ChatGPT, Codex, connected services, or web sources is governed by the applicable provider's terms and privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

D.Jatoliya does not operate a separate Transition Radar datastore from which users can request deletion. For information retained by ChatGPT or another host service, use that service's privacy and data-control mechanisms.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the plugin's data practices change.

Contact

Support is provided through the published Transition Radar support channel.
